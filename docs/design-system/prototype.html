How it works

Three steps, in order.

01Upload

PDF or Word. Fields, signature lines and parties are detected by rules and by AI, then shown to you on the page.

02Approve

Move, relabel or add anything. Approval locks the version; every later change becomes a new version with its own hash.

03Send

One client or a spreadsheet of hundreds. Reminders, countersigning and the final certificate happen without you.
